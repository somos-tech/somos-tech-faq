Why can't I access SOMOS.tech from my location?
We're currently rolling out SOMOS.tech in phases, starting with select regions. This allows us to maintain high-quality services, manage infrastructure costs, and ensure the best experience for our community. We appreciate your patience as we work to expand globally.

We're actively evaluating new regions for expansion. While we don't have specific timelines yet, we're committed to growing our reach. Stay tuned to our social channels for announcements about new region availability.

❓ General Questions

What is SOMOS.tech?
SOMOS.tech is a community platform for Hispanic and Latinx professionals and allies in tech. We provide mentorship, networking, career resources, and community programs to help our members thrive in the technology industry.
Is SOMOS.tech free to join?
Yes! SOMOS.tech offers free membership with access to our community features, networking opportunities, and many of our programs. Some premium features and specialized programs may have associated costs.
How can I contact SOMOS.tech support?
For support inquiries, please reach out to us through our main website at somos.tech or connect with us on our social media channels.
